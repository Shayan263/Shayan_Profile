Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers cookies and similar technologies, including local storage. The site code uses browser storage even if your browser does not show a conventional cookie.

1. Browser storage used by the site

  • shayan-theme: remembers your light/dark theme preference.
  • shayanPortfolioVisitorCompleted: remembers that you completed or skipped the optional visitor prompt.
  • shayanPortfolioAnalyticsConsent: remembers your analytics choice.
  • shayanPortfolioVisitorId: a random identifier used only if you opt in to analytics; it is removed when you withdraw consent.
  • shayanResumeBuilder: stores a resume draft locally if you choose “Save Draft” in the Resume Builder.

2. Optional analytics

Analytics is off until you choose “Accept analytics.” “Reject analytics” and “Continue without analytics” both keep it off and do not block the portfolio. Your visitor-category choice is separate from analytics consent. Use “Privacy settings” in the footer to change your choice later.

When enabled, analytics events are sent to Supabase and a Google Apps Script endpoint. Changing your choice stops future events but does not automatically delete records already sent to those services.

3. Third-party image

The QR code image is loaded from api.qrserver.com. That request can disclose technical request information to the image provider, independently of your analytics choice.

4. Managing browser storage

You can clear this site's local storage and site data in your browser settings. Clearing browser storage may reset the theme, visitor prompt, analytics preference, and locally saved resume draft. It does not delete records held by external providers.

5. Updates

This policy will be updated if the site adds or removes storage keys, analytics, embeds, or other third-party resources.

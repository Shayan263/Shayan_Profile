Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Syed Shayan Ali handles information when you visit the professional portfolio at shayan263.github.io/Shayan_Profile.

1. Who operates the site

The site is operated by Syed Shayan Ali. For privacy questions or requests, email syedshayan.ali.330@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. This is a personal portfolio; it does not sell products, subscriptions, or paid services.

2. Information processed

Optional analytics: only after you choose “Accept analytics,” the site may send event type, page path, referring host or campaign source, broad device category, and a visitor category if you selected one. A random browser identifier is created only for opted-in analytics. The site does not intentionally send your name, email address, message contents, precise location, or screen width through these analytics events.

Analytics events are sent to Supabase and a Google Apps Script endpoint. If you reject analytics or withdraw consent, the page stops sending new analytics events and removes the local analytics identifier. Events already sent to a provider are not automatically deleted by changing your browser setting; email me if you want to request deletion.

Browser storage: the site stores a theme preference, whether you completed the optional visitor prompt, your analytics choice, and (if you use the Resume Builder and save a draft) resume details in local storage on your device. The Resume Builder draft is not sent to the site by the builder code.

Contact: if you contact me by email or choose to open a social service, those services process information according to their own policies.

3. Third-party resources

The portfolio displays a QR image loaded from api.qrserver.com. When your browser loads that image, the QR service may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. Hosting is provided through GitHub Pages. External links may take you to services with their own privacy practices.

4. Purposes and choices

Information is used to operate the portfolio, understand optional site usage, maintain functionality, and respond to direct enquiries. Choosing “Recruiter,” “Referral,” or “Just Exploring” is not analytics consent. You can reject analytics and still use the portfolio. Use “Privacy settings” in the footer to change your choice later.

5. Retention and deletion

Browser storage remains until the site removes it or you clear site data. Analytics records stored by Supabase or Google Apps Script follow the storage and deletion controls configured in those services. The site does not currently publish a fixed automatic expiry period for those provider-side records. You may email a deletion request; removal may require action in each service, and records already copied or retained by a provider may not disappear immediately.

6. Security and changes

Reasonable care should be taken to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may change when the site or its data practices change; the update date will be revised for material changes.
